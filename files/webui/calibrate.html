Connect any standard USB webcam, print the fixed AprilTag board, and follow the guided capture steps. The same 24 marker IDs are used every time.

How it works
Three steps
  1. Download the A4 landscape PDF and print at 100% scale. Do not fit or shrink it to the page.
  2. Move the AprilTag board around the camera view. Keep every tag sharp and visible.
  3. Capture 10 or more views from different distances and angles, then save.
Standard UVC webcams are supported, including cameras that do not report USB metadata.
